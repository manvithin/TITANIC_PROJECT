Engine room
No secrets
01

Drop Cabin

What: remove the column entirely.

Why: 687 of 891 values (77%) are missing. Filling it would invent a deck for most passengers and add noise.

02

Median for Age

What: fill 177 missing ages with 28.0.

Why: the median is robust to outliers. A few very old passengers pull the mean upward; the median stays typical.

03

Mode for Embarked

What: fill 2 missing ports with S (Southampton).

Why: it is categorical, and the mode is the most frequent and safest replacement.

04

FamilySize and IsAlone

What: FamilySize = SibSp + Parch + 1; IsAlone = 1 when the family size is 1.

Why: travelling context matters. Alone passengers survived at about 30%, versus 51% with family.

05

One-hot encoding

What: turn Sex into Sex_male and Embarked into Embarked_Q / Embarked_S.

Why: categories have no numeric order. drop_first=True avoids duplicate opposite columns.

06

Exclude identifiers

What: drop Name, Ticket and PassengerId.

Why: they are free text or row numbers and do not generalise to new passengers.

07

80/20 split

What: 712 passengers train the model; 179 unseen passengers test it.

Why: enough data to learn, enough held-out data for a stable accuracy estimate.

08

100-tree Random Forest

What: 100 decision trees vote on each prediction.

Why: averaging reduces the overfitting of a single tree, handles mixed features and needs no scaling.

Ice warnings · Limitations

What the model still gets wrong

  • It misses {{ metrics.cm[1][0] }} of the {{ metrics.class_1['support'] | int }} survivors (recall {{ '%.2f' | format(metrics.class_1['recall']) }} for that class).
  • The dataset is small: 891 rows, and only 179 test rows.
  • Median age imputation creates an artificial spike at 28.
  • The split seed (42) and forest seed (847) were chosen to match the published report; changing them changes the metrics slightly.
  • Impurity-based feature importance is useful for intuition, not proof of causation.
Course corrections

How to improve it

  • Cross-validation and hyperparameter tuning (max_depth, min_samples_leaf).
  • Extract titles such as Mr / Mrs / Miss from Name.
  • Add cabin-deck or "has cabin" features, and family survival signals.
  • Tune the decision threshold for higher survivor recall.
  • Try gradient boosting (XGBoost or LightGBM) for a stronger baseline.
