Below Decks · Engine Room

From raw rows to a prediction

Every choice is simple enough to explain in a live review.

  1. 01

    Clean

    Drop Cabin (77% missing), fill Embarked with the mode S, fill Age with the median 28.

  2. 02

    Engineer

    Add FamilySize = SibSp + Parch + 1 and IsAlone for travelling context.

  3. 03

    Encode

    One-hot encode Sex and Embarked; drop Name, Ticket and PassengerId.

  4. 04

    Split

    Hold out 20%: 712 passengers for training and 179 for an honest test.

  5. 05

    Train

    Grow 100 decision trees on bootstrapped samples and let them vote.

  6. 06

    Evaluate

    Measure accuracy, the confusion matrix, precision, recall and F1.
